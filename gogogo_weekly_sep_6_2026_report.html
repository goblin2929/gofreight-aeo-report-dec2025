Executive Summary

Period in Review: a strong rebound, led by recovered brand demand and a working PDP program

Combined clicks rose to 134,979 (+36.49% vs the prior week), recovering last week's pullback and setting a new high. Branded demand led the recovery (+92.6% combined), and the organic pages grew alongside it:

  • Indonesia +26.1%, led by brand (+104.3%, CTR 26.0% to 48.1%). The organic side also grew: PDP +12.1%, driven by the Roblox Robux voucher PDP we flagged now ranking position 3.3 (0 to 3,563 clicks), with Mobile Legends (+2,996) and Free Fire (+1,348) recovering and Magic Chess holding its gains.
  • Philippines +81.5%, a broad rebound across every segment (branded +79.6%, non-branded +90.9%, PDP +94.7%) with position improving 5.78 to 4.61. The Poppo Live PDP led (position 3.9 to 2.9); Mobile Legends PH nearly tripled.
  • Malaysia -20.1%, on a tiny base (324 to 259); week to week swings are large and not very meaningful on a market this small, and the PDPs actually grew +8.1%.
  • The pattern is brand and navigational demand recovering across the two large markets, with organic pages compounding the gains, most clearly the Roblox PDP targeting fix and the Philippine Poppo cluster.

Indonesia rebounded 26.1%: brand demand recovered and the organic program delivered

Indonesia rebounded 26.1% (79,737 to 100,546), recovering last week's pullback. The largest single driver is brand: branded clicks more than doubled, +104.3% (19,432 to 39,694), with the brand CTR jumping 26.0% to 48.1%. This is demand and navigational search returning after last week's dip, not a page change.

The organic side also grew, and the PDP program is clearly working. The PDP page cut rose +12.1% (36,722 to 41,171), led by the Roblox Robux voucher PDP we flagged for a targeting fix: it went from 0 to 3,563 clicks and now ranks position 3.3, so the Roblox cluster finally lands on its own PDP instead of the homepage. Mobile Legends (+2,996 clicks) and Free Fire (+1,348) recovered on returning demand, and the Magic Chess Go Go PDP we optimized held its gains near the top.

Non-branded (a query cut) read roughly flat at +0.9% while PDP (a page cut) grew +12.1%: the Roblox recovery landed on the PDP through queries GSC anonymises, so it shows in the page view but not the query view. Overall position eased slightly (5.16 to 5.63) as more long tail impressions returned to the pool.
