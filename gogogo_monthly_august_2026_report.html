Executive Summary

Month in Review: a strong August, led by organic and product page growth

Combined clicks rose to 475,016 (+20.81% vs July), the strongest month this quarter. The growth was organic led: non-branded +32.4% and the PDP page cut +34.9% combined, both outpacing brand (+11.0%), so the traffic gain is increasingly earned rather than navigational:

  • Indonesia +22.6%, organic led: non-branded +32.9% and PDP +35.0% outpaced brand (+8.7%), with overall position flat (5.34 to 5.31) and the blog up +9.8%.
  • Philippines +18.5%, the same pattern: non-branded +29.2% and PDP +38.2% outpaced brand (+17.0%). Position eased 3.83 to 4.84 as more long tail terms entered the pool, but clicks grew regardless.
  • Malaysia -26.8%, on a small base (6,517 to 4,773), a mostly demand driven brand decline; the PDPs held (+1.2%).
  • The through line is the PDP optimization program compounding: across both large markets, product and non-branded pages grew faster than branded demand, which is the healthiest shape of growth.

Indonesia grew 22.6% in August, led by the organic and PDP program

Indonesia grew 22.6% month over month (292,782 to 359,030 clicks). The growth is organic led: non-branded clicks rose +32.9% (168,257 to 223,660) and the PDP page cut rose +35.0% (104,418 to 140,980), while brand grew a steadier +8.7% (124,525 to 135,370). Overall position held flat (5.34 to 5.31).

This is the clearest monthly evidence that the PDP optimization program is compounding: the non-branded and product pages are now growing faster than branded demand, so more of the traffic is being earned rather than navigational. The blog also grew +9.8% (46,679 to 51,263).
