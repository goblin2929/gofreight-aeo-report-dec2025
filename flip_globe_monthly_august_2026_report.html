Executive Summary

Month in Review: a softer August for the converter and blog, with Globe the bright spot

August was a demand softer month across the converter and blog areas, coming off a strong July. Positions eased only modestly, so this reads as seasonal demand softening on the head currency pairs rather than a ranking loss:

  • Flip Globe +2.1% (1,333 to 1,361), the one area that grew; the aggregate position eased 8.47 to 10.12 on the anonymized long tail.
  • Converter Indonesian -21.0% (22,128 to 17,470), the largest area by volume, on softer demand for the head pairs (dollar to rupiah and similar); its position eased only slightly (6.83 to 7.08).
  • Converter English -21.3% (996 to 784) on small absolute numbers, position 9.31 to 10.92.
  • Whole-site blog -5.0% (133,464 to 126,769); the globe-related blog subset eased in line (7,544 to 7,144).

Trend note: the most recent weeks (late August into early September) show Converter English and Flip Globe recovering, so the exit rate into September is more positive than the August month total suggests.

What happened to Globe: modest click growth, the one area up in August

Globe clicks grew +2.1% (1,333 to 1,361), the only Flip area to grow month over month. The aggregate position eased 8.47 to 10.12, but that sits in the anonymized long tail rather than the click drivers, so it is not a reliable signal on its own.

GSC caveat: most Globe impressions are anonymized, so the position is a page level figure; the modest click growth is the reliable read for the month, and the priority destinations (Malaysia, China, Singapore, Australia, Philippines) remain the focus for the next push.

Globe position diagnosis: the page average eased on breadth, not on the money keywords

Three Globe pages show an easing average position in August: /en/globe/malaysia (9.90 to 13.84), /en/globe (14.57 to 25.53) and /id/globe/china (8.30 to 9.55). All three grew clicks in the same month (+31%, +1% and +59%), so this is not a traffic loss. GSC anonymises 82 to 90% of impressions on these pages, and in August each page began surfacing for a wider set of generic long tail transfer queries at position 30 and below, which dilutes the impression weighted page average even though the queries the pages are actually clicked on held.

Is it the main keyword? No. The anchor keyword on all three is flat or better: flip globe 2.4 to 2.3 and kirim uang ke china 6.7 to 6.2 both improved, and flip on the Malaysia page is essentially flat (4.7 to 5.3). No money keyword lost rank.

What happened to Converter: a demand softer August, positions broadly held

Converter Indonesian eased 21.0% (22,128 to 17,470), the largest area by volume, on softer demand for the head pairs (dollar to rupiah and similar) coming off a strong July. Its segment position eased only slightly (6.83 to 7.08), so this is demand rather than a ranking loss.

Converter English eased 21.3% (996 to 784) on small absolute numbers, position 9.31 to 10.92. The head page usd-usa-idr remains around page two, still the largest untapped ceiling: pulling the head English pairs (usd to idr, sgd to idr) onto page one is where the English converter's growth sits.
